Recruitment fraud alert

Accenture does not charge fees to candidates for employment, training, or placement at any stage of recruitment.

Accenture does not authorize agents to collect money on its behalf. Fraudsters may impersonate Accenture recruiters using fake offer letters.

Official Accenture communication uses @accenture.com email domains. Be cautious of offers from free email services claiming to represent Accenture.

Accenture does not conduct interviews exclusively through WhatsApp or Telegram. Verify roles on accenture.com/careers.

Do not share Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, or pay any amount before verifying the opportunity through official Accenture channels.
