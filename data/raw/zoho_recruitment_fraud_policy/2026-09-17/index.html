Recruitment fraud alert

Zoho does not charge candidates registration fees, training fees, or security deposits for campus or lateral hiring.

Zoho does not authorize third parties to collect money for employment. Fraudsters frequently target Zoho campus hiring in Tamil Nadu and across India.

Legitimate Zoho communication uses @zohocorp.com email domains. Offers from Gmail, Yahoo, or WhatsApp-only channels are not official.

Apply through https://www.zoho.com/careers/ and official Zoho recruitment portals only.
