Recruitment fraud alert

HCLTech does not charge money, registration fees, or security deposits from candidates at any stage of recruitment.

HCLTech does not authorize third parties to collect fees for employment. Fraudulent agencies may misuse the HCLTech or HCL name and logo.

Legitimate HCLTech communication uses official domains such as @hcl.com and @hcltech.com. Be alert to job offers from free email services claiming to be HCLTech.

HCLTech does not conduct interviews exclusively on WhatsApp or Telegram. Apply through official HCLTech careers channels only.

Verify opportunities at https://www.hcltech.com/careers before sharing personal documents or making payments.
