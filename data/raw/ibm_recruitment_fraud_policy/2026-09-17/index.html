Recruitment fraud alert

IBM does not charge candidates fees for employment, interviews, training, or background checks as part of legitimate hiring.

IBM never requests payment via gift cards, cryptocurrency, UPI, or wire transfer for job opportunities.

Legitimate IBM recruiters use @ibm.com email addresses. Offers from Gmail, Yahoo, or other free mailboxes claiming to be IBM are suspicious.

IBM does not conduct official interviews solely via WhatsApp or Telegram. Verify roles at ibm.com/careers.

Report suspected IBM recruitment fraud through official IBM careers channels.
