        Important - Recruitment fraud alert

        Kindly be watchful and do not respond to any individuals/entities who demand a fees/charge during the recruitment process with KPMG in India
        event 5 December 2024

        Share

        We would like to bring to your notice that KPMG in India follows a merit-based employee recruitment practice with extensive screening steps. KPMG in India does not charge/accept any amount or security deposit from job seekers during the recruitment process, nor has it authorised any individual/entity to charge such fees/money on its behalf.

        It has come to our knowledge that certain fraudulent individuals/placement agencies, falsely claiming to be the empaneled vendors/employees of KPMG in India are enticing and misleading job aspirants with employment offers for a fee/security deposit. These fraudulent individuals/agencies use the name/logo of KPMG in India in an unauthorised manner and demand money by way of fees, charges etc. from aspiring candidates for procuring employment with KPMG in India.

        Disclaimer: How to identify Recruitment Fraud

        • KPMG in India never requests for fees for any purpose during or after the hiring process. KPMG in India will never request money for the opportunity to apply or work
        • The hiring process involves at least one interview (virtual or in-person). KPMG in India will not request for an interview on any instant messaging applications
        • The KPMG in India Recruitment team corresponds from email addresses with the domain “@kpmg.com”. However, there is also a possibility of scammers to display the sent email domain address as “@kpmg.com” fraudulently. Nevertheless, receiving emails will be possible only with the legitimate “@kpmg.com” domain. In case any generic email ID ending is copied while receiving a job offer or interview call, please be alert on the likelihood of a scammer
        • The KPMG Recruitment team does not request or require personal documents like bank account details, tax forms or credit card information as part of the recruitment process.

        We request you to be watchful and not respond to any individuals/entities who demand a fees/charge during the recruitment process with KPMG in India, as we do not charge any money fees from anyone seeking a job with us. In case of any suspicion, you are encouraged to report the incident directly to us.
