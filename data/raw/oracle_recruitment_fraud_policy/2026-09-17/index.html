Recruitment fraud alert

Oracle does not charge candidates any fee for employment or require payment for interviews, training, or equipment.

Oracle does not authorize third parties to collect money for job placement. Fraudsters may impersonate Oracle recruiters.

Legitimate Oracle communication uses @oracle.com email domains. Be cautious of offers from free email services.

Oracle does not conduct interviews exclusively on WhatsApp or Telegram. Verify opportunities at oracle.com/careers.

Do not share sensitive identity or banking documents before verifying the role through official Oracle channels.
