Recruitment fraud alert

Freshworks does not charge candidates any fee at any stage of the recruitment process.

Freshworks does not authorize agencies to collect registration or training fees. Fraudsters may impersonate Freshworks recruiters in India.

Legitimate Freshworks communication uses @freshworks.com email addresses. Be alert to Gmail or WhatsApp-only interview requests.

Verify opportunities at https://www.freshworks.com/company/careers/ before sharing personal documents.
