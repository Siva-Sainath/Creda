Recruitment fraud alert

Wipro does not charge any fee or security deposit from candidates at any stage of the recruitment process.

Wipro does not authorize any agency or individual to collect money for employment. Fraudsters may misuse the Wipro name, logo, or fake offer letters.

Legitimate Wipro communication uses official Wipro email domains such as @wipro.com. Be cautious of offers from Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, or other free email services claiming to be Wipro.

Wipro does not conduct interviews solely via WhatsApp or Telegram. Official hiring follows application through careers.wipro.com, screening, interviews, and formal offer letters.

Verify opportunities only through https://careers.wipro.com/ and official Wipro careers channels.

Report suspected recruitment fraud to Wipro through official careers contact channels.
