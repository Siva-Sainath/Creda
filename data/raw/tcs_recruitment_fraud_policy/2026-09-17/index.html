    Recruitment Fraud Alert: Know the Risks

    We have observed some misleading communication on various portals, fraud emails about TCS hiring processes, and issues of candidates facing spoofing.

    This is to notify you that TCS does not ask for any sort of payment or security deposit from candidates at any stage of the recruitment process. The firm never sends out job offers from free internet email services like Gmail, Rediff Mail, Yahoo Mail, Hotmail, and so on. TCS bears no responsibility for the amount deposited by any candidate/individual in response to scammers and frauds.

    As a vigilant job seeker, keep your eyes open and protect your interests!

    Before trusting your recruiter, note the following: TCS never asks for money or security deposits Sends hiring emails via domain (@tcs.com) only Never shared their employee ID cards Allows candidates to validate their "IT" job offers TCS Helpline for recruitment fraud or queries: Phone Number - 1800-209-3111 Email Id - careers@tcs.com

    Recruitment Fraud Alert

    Unmasking the Frauds Around You

    Imposter job, internship, or training offers can be quite tough to identify unless you remain informed about the common ways scammers use to exploit the interest of job seekers.

    Fake Job Agencies

    Many fraudulent recruitment agencies claim to help job seekers find employment, provide training, offer internships, or promise interviews by charging exorbitant fees for their services. Such agencies do not have any real opportunities and are only interested in collecting fees from unsuspecting job seekers.

    Social Media Job Scams

    Scammers create fake social media profiles to advertise job openings, internship offers, and training opportunities to appear legitimate. Often, they impersonate TCS and use the reference of real employees to lend credibility to their scams. 

    Imposter Job Vacancies On Verified Job Portals

    Many reputable portals consist of legitimate and fraudulent employment opportunities that could be complex to differentiate for a job seeker. Since a candidate already has an established trust in the job portal, scammers post fake openings to gain money.

    Online Test Scams

    Quite commonly, scammers propose online tests claiming to offer high-paying positions in TCS. Nevertheless, they might end up stealing your identity by selling your personal information and tricking you into paying numerous sorts of charges.  

    Email Job Scams

    Many scheming emails might land up in your inbox offering employment opportunities. The purpose of this type of email is to deceive individuals into divulging personal information by posing as a legitimate entity.

    Unsolicited Messages

    Scammers often offer fake jobs and interview at TCS, promise to provide training, or falsely claim your selection through unsolicited messages on messaging applications like SMS, WhatsApp, or Telegram to deceive job seekers.

    Such fake offers are often followed by registration forms/admit cards, interview calls, appointment letters, and official-looking email IDs that might appear legitimate to candidates but unfortunately could be a trap.

    Take a look at the different fraudulent materials used by scammers to appear legitimate and deceive job seekers: Click here

    TCS never asks for any money or security offering job offers, interviews, training, opportunities, or internships. TCS does not approach through messaging apps, unofficial emails, social media, job portals, training, or agencies We advise you to check the authenticity of the offer before progressing further.

    Received an offer letter?

    As a preventive measure, Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) has protected offer letters for all “IT” hires through an offer verification tool. TCS suggests you verify the offer letter received before acting on any request of depositing money and adhering to further requests from the recruiter.

    1. Click on the button to validate your offer/joining/provisional letter
    2. Enter your reference ID and captcha on the screen
    3. Validate the letter through a Mobile QR Code Scanner
