Recruitment fraud alert

Razorpay does not charge candidates any fee for employment, training, or onboarding.

Razorpay does not authorize third parties to collect money for job placement. Fraudsters may misuse the Razorpay brand in India startup hiring scams.

Legitimate Razorpay communication uses @razorpay.com email domains. Offers from free email services or WhatsApp-only interviews are suspicious.

Apply only through https://razorpay.com/jobs/ and official Razorpay careers channels.
