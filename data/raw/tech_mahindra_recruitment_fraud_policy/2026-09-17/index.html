Recruitment fraud alert

Tech Mahindra does not charge any registration fee, security deposit, or training fee from candidates at any stage of hiring.

Tech Mahindra does not authorize agencies to collect money for employment. Fraudsters may use fake offer letters and impersonate recruiters.

Official Tech Mahindra communication uses @techmahindra.com email domains. Be alert to offers from free email services.

Tech Mahindra does not conduct interviews exclusively on WhatsApp or Telegram. Apply through careers.techmahindra.com.

Report suspected recruitment fraud through official Tech Mahindra careers contact channels.
