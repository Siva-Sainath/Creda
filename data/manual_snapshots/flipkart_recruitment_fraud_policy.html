Recruitment fraud alert

Flipkart does not charge candidates any fee for internships, campus hiring, or full-time employment.

Flipkart does not authorize agents to collect registration fees, training fees, or security deposits. Internship scams targeting students are common in India.

Legitimate Flipkart communication uses @flipkart.com email addresses. Offers from Gmail, WhatsApp-only contacts, or Telegram groups are not official.

Flipkart hiring requires application through flipkartcareers.com followed by formal interview and offer processes.

Verify all Flipkart opportunities only through https://www.flipkartcareers.com/ before sharing documents or making payments.
