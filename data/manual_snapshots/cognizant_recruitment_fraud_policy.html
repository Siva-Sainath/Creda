Recruitment fraud alert

Cognizant does not charge candidates any fee at any stage of the hiring process, including application, interview, or onboarding.

Cognizant never requests payment for training, equipment, background checks, or job placement through unofficial channels.

Legitimate Cognizant recruiters communicate from @cognizant.com email addresses. Offers from Gmail, Yahoo, or other free mailboxes should be treated with suspicion.

Cognizant does not conduct official interviews solely via WhatsApp or Telegram. Apply through careers.cognizant.com.

Report suspected recruitment fraud to Cognizant through official careers contact channels.
