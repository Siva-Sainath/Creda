Recruitment fraud alert

Microsoft is committed to protecting job seekers from recruitment fraud and impersonation scams.

Microsoft does not charge fees for applications, interviews, training, visas, equipment, or background checks. Microsoft recruiters do not ask for gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfers, or banking information as part of hiring.

Legitimate Microsoft recruiting email comes from @microsoft.com addresses. Be alert to look-alike domains and free webmail senders claiming to represent Microsoft.

Microsoft does not conduct hiring interviews exclusively through WhatsApp, Telegram, or other instant messaging apps without verified official process.

Verify roles on https://careers.microsoft.com/ before sharing personal information.

Report suspected recruitment fraud through Microsoft's official fraud reporting channels.
