Recruitment fraud alert

Google is aware that unauthorized individuals and organizations may attempt to scam job seekers by impersonating Google recruiters.

Google does not charge candidates any fee at any stage of the hiring process. Google does not ask for payment for interviews, training, equipment, visas, or application processing.

Legitimate Google recruiting communications come from email addresses ending in @google.com. Be suspicious of offers sent from free email services such as Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, or Outlook when the sender claims to represent Google hiring.

Google conducts interviews through official processes. Be cautious of interview requests sent only through messaging apps such as WhatsApp or Telegram.

Do not share banking details, government ID numbers, or passwords before verifying the recruiter through official Google careers channels.

If you receive a suspicious message claiming to be from Google, do not pay money or share sensitive information. Verify opportunities at https://careers.google.com/
