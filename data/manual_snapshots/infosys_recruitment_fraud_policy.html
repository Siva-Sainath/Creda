Recruitment fraud alert

Infosys does not charge money or accept security deposits from candidates at any stage of the recruitment process.

Infosys does not authorize third parties to collect fees for employment. Fraudulent agencies may misuse the Infosys name and logo.

Legitimate Infosys communication uses official Infosys domains. Be cautious of job offers from free email services such as Gmail, Yahoo, or Hotmail claiming to be Infosys.

Infosys hiring is merit-based with formal interview processes. Be alert to requests for payment, training fees, or document charges.

Verify opportunities through https://www.infosys.com/careers.html and official Infosys careers channels only.

Report suspected recruitment fraud to Infosys through official careers contact channels.
