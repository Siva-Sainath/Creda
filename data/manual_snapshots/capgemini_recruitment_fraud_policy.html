Recruitment fraud alert

Capgemini does not charge candidates any fee for recruitment, training, or job placement.

Capgemini does not authorize third parties to collect money for employment. Fraudulent actors may misuse the Capgemini brand.

Legitimate Capgemini communication uses @capgemini.com email addresses. Offers from Gmail or other free mailboxes are not official.

Capgemini does not conduct interviews solely via WhatsApp or Telegram. Apply through capgemini.com/careers.

Verify all opportunities through official Capgemini careers channels before sharing documents or making payments.
